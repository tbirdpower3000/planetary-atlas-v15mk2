Hand control training

About a minute of exercises that teach the Planetary Modeling Atlas how your hand moves, so the cursor reaches the whole screen, your pinch is read reliably, and buttons are as easy to hit as you need.

  1. Reach: move your open hand to four comfortable corners.
  2. Pinch: open your hand, then pinch thumb to index finger, a few times.
  3. Hold still: keep the cursor on a dot for three seconds.
  4. Targets: press eight buttons as they appear.
